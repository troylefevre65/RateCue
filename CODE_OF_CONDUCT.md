# Code of conduct

RateCue welcomes listeners, developers, and hardware reviewers of every background and experience level.

- Be respectful and assume good faith. Critique evidence and code, not people.
- Keep discussion on topic. Support claims about formats or hardware with reproducible observations.
- Do not harass, insult, or share anyone's private information.

The maintainer may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation that violates this code. Report concerns privately to the maintainer through the contact options on the [maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/troylefevre65).
